The chemistry

Why we build on LiFePO4.

Lithium iron phosphate is the chemistry buyers trust for backup power — the reasons matter to your customers.

01

Safer under stress

LiFePO4 is thermally more stable than standard lithium-ion, with a much lower risk of thermal runaway — a real concern for hot climates and home use.

02

Long cycle life

The chemistry is rated for far more charge cycles than typical lithium-ion, lowering cost-per-cycle over the product's life. Exact cycle count subject to cell spec.

03

Heat tolerant

Better performance retention at high ambient temperatures than many alternatives — suited to African and Latin American conditions.

04

Deeper usable capacity

LiFePO4 tolerates deep discharge well, so more of the rated capacity is usable day to day without shortening battery life.

Good to know

Questions distributors ask.

Are these LiFePO4 or standard lithium-ion?
Our focus is LiFePO4 (lithium iron phosphate) for its safety and cycle life. Where a specific SKU uses a different cell, we will state it clearly — we do not relabel other chemistries as LiFePO4.
Can you ship lithium batteries to my country?
Yes. Lithium batteries ship by sea as UN3480 Class 9 dangerous goods, with UN38.3 test reports, MSDS and a dangerous-goods declaration. Air freight for samples is possible but restricted. Exact routing and documents are confirmed per order.
What certifications do the products carry?
CE and RoHS are our default compliance baseline. Country-specific certifications (for example SONCAP, SASO, or others) can be arranged for volume orders — availability subject to confirmation.
Do you offer OEM and custom branding?
Yes — logo, colour finish, packaging and specification adjustments are available. Minimums and development timelines depend on the model and are confirmed on request.
What are your prices and terms?
We offer wholesale pricing on request. Send us the models, quantities and destination and we will prepare a quotation. Pricing, lead times and terms are confirmed in writing before any order.
Can I get samples before ordering?
Yes — we recommend testing units in your own market conditions first. Sample terms are confirmed per request.
