ABOUT ANTHRISE

Clean energy for everyday life and lasting progress.

Approx. 7,000 m² factory

We believe the value of energy lies in the difference it makes to people's lives. From everyday electricity at home to business continuity and outdoor lighting, accessible and efficiently used clean energy can create lasting value for society.

ANTHRISE focuses on solar equipment and energy systems, covering the generation, conversion, storage and use of energy. Our range includes battery storage, solar panels, inverters, solar lighting and supporting components. We aim to turn technical expertise into practical products, giving homes and businesses more flexible energy choices and helping distributors, installers and project partners find combinations suited to their markets and applications.

Our approximately 200-person team spans research and development, production and sales, supported by a factory of approximately 7,000 square metres. Our R&D team connects product design with application needs. Production and sales work together to bring products to market and feed customer experience back into development and manufacturing.

Customer needs guide us. Expertise and quality support our work. Integrity builds lasting partnerships.

These principles shape how we work. We value clear communication, attention to product details and responsibility for delivery commitments, while listening to how products perform in use. We aim to be a partner who helps customers solve problems and create ongoing value. Through wider use of solar energy, we hope to improve energy efficiency and bring clean energy to more places where people live and work.

06 / FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

A few common questions about sizing, delivery and cooperation.

Where can 16 kWh storage be used?

It can be an initial capacity reference for home backup, solar-plus-storage or selected commercial uses. Suitability depends on load, peaks, inverter configuration and grid requirements.

Do you cover inverters and PV modules?

Inverters, PV modules and related components are part of ANTHRISE's business scope. Actual options, certifications, compatibility and delivery terms are project-specific.

Is the calculator a runtime guarantee?

No. It is a simplified estimate based on capacity, usable fraction, conversion efficiency and constant load. Actual results depend on environment, equipment efficiency, load variation and BMS behavior.
