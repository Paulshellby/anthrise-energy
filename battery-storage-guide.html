16kWh vs 32kWh Battery Storage: Capacity and Model Guide

A kWh product name describes a capacity class. Selection depends on the model’s listed nominal energy, physical format and system requirements. These three ANTHRISE models illustrate the differences.

ModelFormatNominal energyNominal voltage
AE-E-DAWN-16Floor stand15.36 kWh51.2 V
AE-E-READY-16Pre-assembled16.0768 kWh51.2 V
AE-E-READY-32Home storage32.15 kWh51.2 V

Why do two 16kWh models list different energy values?

AE-E-DAWN-16 is listed at 51.2V and 300Ah, giving 15.36kWh. The shown AE-E-READY-16 configuration is 51.2V and 314Ah, giving 16.0768kWh. They occupy a similar capacity class but are different products.

How should you compare backup runtime?

List the loads that must stay on and your target backup time. Nominal energy is different from usable energy. Review usable capacity, conversion efficiency, output rating, starting loads, temperature and ageing conditions when sizing a system.

What should you prepare for a project discussion?

  1. Installation region, indoor or outdoor environment and available space.
  2. Existing inverter model, system voltage and communications requirements.
  3. Continuous loads, starting loads and target backup duration.

This guide follows the current product configuration records. Confirm the supplied configuration, compatibility and installation design during project review.
